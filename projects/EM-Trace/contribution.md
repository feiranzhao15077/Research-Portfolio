# EM-Trace contribution

_Contribution summary for mentor-facing discussion._

---

## 🛠️ Engineering contribution

The project builds an end-to-end chain from SolidWorks parameterized parts and assembly through STEP AP214 exchange, CST frequency-domain solving, complex far-field extraction and Python slow-time/STFT processing.

## 🔍 Audit contribution

It binds direction conventions, polarization basis, mesh level, calculation domain, logs, artifacts and result identities into a reviewable evidence chain. Direction, mesh and domain problems are treated as diagnostic findings rather than hidden implementation details.

## ⚠️ Evidence-discipline contribution

The project distinguishes engineering capability, limited numerical baseline and process demonstration. This preserves useful workflow evidence while preventing unconverged RCS or historical forward-angle inputs from being presented as validated backscatter micro-Doppler results.

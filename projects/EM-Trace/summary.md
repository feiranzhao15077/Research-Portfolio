# EM-Trace summary

_Portfolio summary derived from the EM-Trace frozen project summary._

---

## 🎯 Project objective

EM-Trace is a parameterized CAD and full-wave electromagnetic simulation engineering project for a simplified quadrotor target. It connects geometry construction, STEP AP214 exchange, CST frequency-domain solving, complex far-field extraction, slow-time signal generation and STFT processing.

## 🔍 What the project demonstrates

The central engineering goal is to bind geometry, excitation, observation direction, complex fields, mesh, logs and result identities into a reviewable chain. When direction, mesh, calculation-domain or provenance issues appeared, the project retained the evidence, designed controls and downgraded claims to the supported evidence level.

## ⚠️ Scientific scope

The current outputs are engineering baselines and process demonstrations. They do not support a claim of high-precision absolute RCS, full numerical convergence or real-drone experimental validation.

_Portfolio summary derived from the existing mentor-facing project materials._

## 🎯 Research focus

EMvision is a reproducible numerical project combining two-dimensional electromagnetic simulation, rotor micro-Doppler analysis and three-dimensional physical-optics angle sequences. Its display narrative is organized around how channel, generator and parameter conditions affect the interpretation of synthetic rotor echoes.

## 🔍 Current scientific scope

The current version is a synthetic study under assumed medium conditions. It does not claim measured radar validation or measured identification performance. High within-condition accuracy is not treated as evidence of cross-condition generalization.

## 📦 Portfolio boundary

This portfolio keeps only a derived summary, methodology, key-result explanation, contribution note and selected figures. The original project remains the source of detailed scripts, logs, data and paper materials.

# EMvision contribution

_Contribution summary for mentor-facing discussion._

---

## 🎯 Technical contribution

The project integrates electromagnetic simulation, rotor micro-Doppler processing, three-dimensional PO angle sequences and feature-identification analysis into one reproducible numerical workflow.

## 🔍 Evidence contribution

The display version makes the channel–generator–parameter attribution structure explicit, includes negative-control and robustness checks, and adds resource-allocation and third-generator diagnostics without changing the main estimand.

## ⚠️ Scientific practice contribution

An important part of the work is the explicit withdrawal of unsupported CST and unconverged numerical claims. The portfolio therefore presents the project as synthetic, evidence-bounded research rather than as measured validation.

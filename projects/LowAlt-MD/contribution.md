## 🎯 Mechanism contribution

The project turns the question of “multipath changing amplitude versus structure” into an auditable sequence from element-wise path phase to coherent full-target interference and normalized micro-Doppler change.

## 🔍 Recognition contribution

The project explicitly separates synthetic energy shortcuts from controlled structural features, adds label-permutation negative controls, and reports cross-height effects without collapsing directional domain shift into a universal monotonic claim.

## 🧭 Boundary contribution

The matched-budget R3 protocol makes a cautious generalization statement: the tested simple multi-environment combination did not yield a stable advantage. The result is presented as a boundary of the current protocol, not as a universal verdict on domain generalization.
